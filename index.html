About

Professional summary

I am an HR and payroll professional with more than 14 years in the UAE construction and engineering sector. My work covers the full employee lifecycle, from onboarding to final settlement, with payroll accuracy and regulatory compliance at the centre.

Alongside core HR, I hold an MBA in Data Science & Analytics. I build Power BI dashboards, use SQL and Python for reporting, and automate repetitive payroll work in Excel VBA. This means fewer manual errors, faster month-end closing, and HR reports that management can act on.

I am now focused on HR Manager-level responsibilities, combining strong operational control with data-driven people decisions.

Accurate salaries, clean compliance and clear data build trust with every employee.My working principle
