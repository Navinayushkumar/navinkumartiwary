About Me

HR leadership with an analytics edge

I am a strategic, data-driven Recruitment and Payroll Specialist with 14+ years in the UAE construction and engineering sector. I run full-cycle talent acquisition, from sourcing and screening to offers and onboarding, alongside multi-site payroll, WPS submission, end-of-service settlements and UAE Labour Law compliance.

With an MBA in Data Science & Analytics, I use Power BI, SQL, Python and advanced Excel to automate reporting and support executive HR decisions. The result is fewer manual errors, faster month-end closing, and reports management can act on.

I am committed to aligning people strategy with business growth in dynamic, multicultural environments.
