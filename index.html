About Me

HR leadership with an analytics edge

I am an HR and payroll professional with more than 14 years in the UAE construction and engineering sector. My work covers the full employee lifecycle, from onboarding to final settlement, with payroll accuracy and regulatory compliance at the centre.

Alongside core HR, I hold an MBA in Data Science & Analytics. I build Power BI dashboards, use SQL and Python for reporting, and automate repetitive payroll work in Excel VBA. The result is fewer manual errors, faster month-end closing, and clear HR reporting.

I am now focused on HR Manager-level responsibilities, combining strong operational control with data-driven people decisions.
