How it's calculated

Who qualifies

Private-sector employees with at least one year of continuous service (Article 51, Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021).

The formula

Daily wage = basic ÷ 30. Then 21 days' wage for each of the first 5 years, and 30 days' wage for each year after that. Part years are paid in proportion.

The limit

Total gratuity can't be more than 24 months of basic salary. Resigning no longer reduces the amount under the current law.

This calculator gives an estimate for general information. Service is counted in calendar days (including both the joining date and the last day) and divided by 365. It doesn't cover free zones with their own rules (such as DIFC and ADGM), UAE and GCC nationals on pension schemes, or money the employee owes the company.
