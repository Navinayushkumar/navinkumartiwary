How it's calculated

Annual leave

Less than 6 months: none yet. 6 to 12 months: 2 days for each full month. After a year: 30 days a year, with part years in proportion (Article 29).

Leave salary

Unused leave is valued at basic salary ÷ 30 per day. Some companies use basic × 12 ÷ 365 instead, so check your policy.

Sick leave

90 days a year after probation: days 1–15 full pay, 16–45 half pay, 46–90 unpaid. During probation, sick leave can only be unpaid (Article 31).

This calculator gives an estimate for general information. It doesn't apply carry-forward limits, company policies that give more than the law, or free zones with their own rules (such as DIFC and ADGM).
