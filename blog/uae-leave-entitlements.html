Leave entitlements under UAE Labour Law: every type explained

Annual, sick, maternity, parental, bereavement and study leave: how many days, how they're paid, and the mistakes to avoid.

By Navin Kumar Tiwary31 Oct 20267 min read

Leave questions come up every week in HR: how many sick days are paid, when maternity leave can start, whether unused annual leave can be cashed in. Here is every type of leave a private-sector employee is entitled to under the UAE Labour Law, in one place.

Annual leave

An employee is entitled to 30 days of paid annual leave for each year of service. Someone who has worked more than 6 months but less than a year gets 2 days for each month worked. In the final year, leave is worked out in proportion to the time served.

  • Timing: the employer sets the leave schedule according to work needs and company policy, and can let the employee take leave in one block or in parts.
  • Carrying leave forward: the Executive Regulations allow an employee to carry forward up to half of the annual leave (15 days) to the next year.
  • Cash in lieu: during employment, the employee and employer can agree to pay unused leave as cash. When employment ends, any leave balance due must be paid, calculated on the basic salary.

Sick leave

After completing probation, an employee can take up to 90 days of sick leave a year, either in one go or at different times. It must be supported by a medical report from an approved health authority.

Days of sick leave in the yearPay
Days 1 to 15Full pay
Days 16 to 45Half pay
Days 46 to 90Unpaid

During probation there is no paid sick leave. The employer can approve unpaid sick leave instead, again with a medical report.

Maternity leave

A female employee is entitled to 60 days of maternity leave: 45 days on full pay and 15 days on half pay. She can start it up to 30 days before the expected delivery date.

  • Illness after pregnancy or delivery: up to 45 extra days without pay, with a medical certificate. They can be taken together or separately.
  • Sick child or child with a disability: 30 extra days on full pay, which can be extended by a further 30 days without pay, with a medical certificate.
  • Nursing breaks: for 6 months after the birth, one or two paid breaks a day for nursing, up to one hour in total.

Parental leave

Either parent, mother or father, gets 5 working days of paid parental leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth. This is separate from maternity leave.

Bereavement leave

  • 5 days on the death of a spouse.
  • 3 days on the death of a parent, child, brother, sister, grandchild or grandparent.

Study leave

An employee with at least 2 years of service who is studying at an accredited educational institution in the UAE gets 10 working days of paid leave a year to sit exams.

Public holidays and national service

Employees get full pay on official public holidays. If they are asked to work on one, they must be given another day off or paid for that day plus at least 50% of the basic wage. UAE nationals called up for national service are entitled to paid leave for it, under the national service law.

Common mistakes I see

  • Paying sick leave during probation, or refusing unpaid sick leave without looking at the medical report.
  • Counting the 90 sick days from the joining date instead of within each year.
  • Mixing up maternity leave (mother only, 60 days) and parental leave (either parent, 5 working days).
  • Paying unused annual leave at the end of service on gross salary instead of basic.
  • Letting employees carry forward more than half their annual leave.

My checklist for leave in payroll

  1. Record every leave type separately in the attendance system, never as general "absence".
  2. Check the probation end date before approving paid sick leave.
  3. Keep a running count of sick days in the year so the full-pay, half-pay and unpaid bands are applied correctly.
  4. Attach the medical certificate or supporting document to every sick, maternity and bereavement leave.
  5. Review annual leave balances each quarter so carry-forward stays within the limit.
  6. Include the leave balance, on basic salary, in every final settlement.

Leave is where employees notice mistakes fastest, because it hits their pay directly. Clear records and a simple monthly check stop most problems before payday.

This article is general information based on Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 and its Executive Regulations for the UAE private sector. Free zones such as DIFC and ADGM have their own rules, and company policies or contracts may give more than the legal minimum. For a specific case, check with MOHRE or a legal advisor.
